# Positioning and Voice

## Core pitch

Midsphere ships the difficult parts of AI agents — agents themselves, tools, models, context, sandboxes, scaling, ops, billing, scoping, scheduling, observability — so developers can focus on the product they're building.

## Analogy

Same shape as Supabase for databases, Clerk for auth, Vercel for deployment. An opinionated integrated platform that replaces "a stack of libraries plus self-hosted infrastructure" with one product.

## Voice

- Direct. No marketing hedging. Say what something is.
- Technical when needed. Don't over-explain to non-technical audiences if the audience is technical.
- Honest about tradeoffs. Don't claim things Midsphere doesn't do.
- Concrete. Specific examples beat abstract claims. "47 of your users tried to attach files" beats "deep insights into user behavior."
- No "powered by AI" or "revolutionary" or "game-changing" language.
- Lowercase product name in copy: "midsphere." with the period sometimes when it fits the visual rhythm.

## Things to call by their names

- Agent (not "AI assistant" or "AI helper" or "intelligent agent")
- Tools (not "actions" or "capabilities" — though "capabilities" is okay in capability lists)
- Knowledge (not "context store" or "memory" — though "context" is fine in technical descriptions)
- Per-user controls (not "user management" — controls is more specific)
- Analytics agent (specific term — it's an agent, not an analytics dashboard)
- Run (an invocation of an agent — not "session" or "task" or "execution" in copy)
- End user (the developer's user, distinguished from "developer" who is Midsphere's user)

## Things not to do

- Don't compare directly with foundation labs as runtime competitors. Midsphere is a different category.
- Don't promise things V1 doesn't ship.
- Don't position as "for vibe coders" exclusively. The audience is broader.
- Don't oversell the analytics agent — it's a feature, but execution quality determines whether it's compelling. Show specific insight examples, don't claim it'll find anything magical.

## Phrases that work

- "Skip the assembly. Ship the agent."
- "The hard part is built. Go ship the feature."
- "We brought the agent. You bring what makes it yours."
- "Different category entirely."
- "What [competitor] doesn't ship."

## Phrases that don't work

- "Powered by AI"
- "Cutting-edge agent infrastructure"
- "Revolutionary platform"
- "10x faster"
- "AI-first" (everything is AI now; the term is meaningless)
